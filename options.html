Automatic article lookups

When enabled, authorC reads article identifiers, author names, publication year, and the current page URL locally. It sends the article’s DOI or title and OpenAlex IDs to OpenAlex to fetch citation rankings.

OpenAlex also receives your IP address and any API key you choose to save. Full articles, cookies, and your browsing-history database are not sent. authorC has no analytics.

Enable once to show the panel automatically on supported article pages. You can pause at any time. Privacy policy
