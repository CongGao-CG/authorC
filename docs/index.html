authorC

Author citations.
Alongside your reading.

A Chrome extension that finds a research article in OpenAlex, ranks its authors by total career citations, and highlights the highest-cited author as Author C.

On the page you’re already reading

  • Enable automatic lookups once, then open an article on its publisher’s website.
  • See author citation totals and follow a link to the matching OpenAlex work.
  • Minimize the panel to keep the leading author’s name and count visible.
  • See ties and unavailable counts explicitly.

The Chrome Web Store release is being prepared. This page does not claim the extension has been published or approved.

Data and privacy

authorC sends an article DOI or title to OpenAlex to retrieve rankings. It has no analytics or separate authorC server. Read the privacy policy for details and controls.

Citation totals reflect OpenAlex’s indexed records and author matching. authorC is an independent project, not an official OpenAlex or publisher product.
